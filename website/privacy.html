Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

1. Zero Unwanted Data Harvesting

PocketDesk is built on a local-first architecture. In Local Mode, all your tasks, notes, calendar events, financial records, and AI prompts are stored strictly in an encrypted database on your local device. No telemetry or hidden tracking is gathered.

2. Optional Supabase Cloud Sync

If you choose to register a Cloud Account (Free Tier 500MB or Pro Tier 10GB), your data is transmitted over TLS 1.3 to your dedicated Supabase database tables (`profiles`, `tasks`, `notes`, `calendar_events`). You retain full ownership and right to delete your cloud data at any time via the Master Profile or Settings screen.

3. AI Integration & API Keys

Pocket AI mascot uses user-configured OpenRouter API keys or local LLM models. Your API keys are encrypted in native device storage (`FlutterSecureStorage`) and never transmitted to PocketDesk servers.

4. Account Deletion & Data Rights

Under the Check Data & Delete Account features in Settings, you can permanently wipe your local Isar database, cloud Supabase profile, and pending requests in one click.
