Frequently Asked Questions

Does PocketDesk require an internet connection?

No. PocketDesk is built offline-first. Your calendar, tasks, and notes are saved directly to a high-performance local database on your device.

How does P2P synchronization work?

Syncing happens directly between devices over your local local area network (LAN) using WebSockets. PocketDesk uses cryptographically generated keys to ensure only authorized devices sync with each other, completely bypassing third-party cloud servers.

Are my files secure?

Yes, all application data is stored in a secure local database (Isar) and protected by biometric authentication (like fingerprint or face unlock) if enabled in settings.

What platforms are supported?

PocketDesk is available for Android and Linux desktop. Both builds are fully native, fast, and feature-rich.
