Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about PocketDesk? Here are answers to common questions about accounts, sync, and privacy.

Q: Is my data really private in Local Mode?

Yes. In Local Account mode, all your tasks, notes, calendar events, and expenses stay strictly on your device in an encrypted Isar NoSQL database. No data is sent to external servers.

Q: How does Supabase Cloud Sync work?

When you register a Cloud Account (Free Tier 500 MB or Pro Tier 10 GB), PocketDesk connects to Supabase database REST APIs over TLS 1.3 to synchronize your data automatically across your authorized devices.

Q: Can I convert my Local account to a Cloud account later?

Yes! In Master Profile, tap "View Cloud Plans", select your plan, and enter your Gmail address and master password. Your local data will convert and sync up to Supabase Cloud automatically.

Q: How does the Pocket AI Mascot work?

The Pocket AI Mascot operates using your own OpenRouter API key or local LLM models. Your API keys are encrypted in device storage and never stored on PocketDesk servers.

Q: How do I completely delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Privacy → Check Data or Delete Account. Tapping "Delete Account" permanently wipes your local Isar database, secure storage keys, and remote Supabase profile records.
