Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to expand its answer.

Is my data really 100% private in Local Account Mode?
Yes! In Local Account mode, all your notes, tasks, calendar events, expenses, and settings remain strictly on your local device in an encrypted Isar NoSQL database. No telemetry, analytics, or remote tracking is transmitted to external servers.
How does Supabase Cloud Synchronization work?
When you register a Cloud Account (Free Tier 500 MB or Pro Tier 10 GB), PocketDesk connects directly to PostgreSQL database REST APIs on Supabase over TLS 1.3 to synchronize your data automatically across your authorized devices.
Can I convert my Local account to a Cloud account later?
Yes! Navigate to Master Profile in the app, tap "View Cloud Plans", select your preferred plan (Free or Pro), and enter your Gmail address and master password. Your local data will automatically convert and sync up to your Supabase Cloud profile.
How does the Pocket AI Mascot Assistant operate?
The Pocket AI Mascot operates using your own OpenRouter API key or local LLM models. Your API keys are encrypted in device storage and never stored on PocketDesk servers. You can choose from models such as GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, or Gemini Flash.
How do I completely wipe my account and stored data?
Go to Settings → Privacy → Check Data or Delete Account. Tapping "Delete Account" permanently wipes your local Isar database, secure storage keys, and remote Supabase profile records in one step.
