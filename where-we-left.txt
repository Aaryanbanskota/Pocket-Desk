PROJECT OVERVIEW
====================================================================

PocketDesk

PocketDesk is an offline-first productivity ecosystem.

It combines:

• Calendar
• Task Manager
• Notes
• Schedule Planner
• Device Synchronization
• Official Website

Users own their data.

Everything works offline.
